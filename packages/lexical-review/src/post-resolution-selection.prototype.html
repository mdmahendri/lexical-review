Where should the selection go?

A throwaway version 3 model for feeling how editor selection and proposal navigation behave after creation, refusal, preview, selective resolution, and batch resolution.

Question: does one route-neutral selection bookmark, remapped through the same semantic transaction as the document, keep every proposal kind predictable without treating Lexical node keys or a proposal card as selection authority?
Candidate contract: the package snapshots both logical selection endpoints before a semantic transaction and returns their remapped successors afterward. It collapses only when the editing intent says to collapse or when accepted content containing both endpoints is removed. Proposal inspection is separate. Resolution never auto-advances; a host explicitly invokes deterministic next/previous navigation if it wants that workflow.
Bookmark, not node key

Accepted-state points carry paragraph identity, offset, and boundary association. Visible proposal content may use a proposal-local point.

Remap both endpoints

Forward/backward range orientation survives. A range collapses only when its semantic successors become the same point.

Inspection is separate

The inspected proposal may change without moving text selection or stealing DOM focus. Resolution clears it and exposes a successor hint.

Routes normalize first

Keyboard, beforeinput, toolbar, and programmatic callers produce the same semantic intent and post-selection disposition.

Free play

Load a proposal-creation outcome, move the logical selection, then preview or resolve it. Every action renders the full model state.

Candidate creation matrix

Proposal kind Post-creation logical selection Why
Insertion Collapsed at the end of new proposal-local content Typing or paste can continue at the authored edge.
Deletion Collapsed at the normalized continuation boundary Backward continues before the marked range; forward continues after it. A range command defaults to its document-order start.
Atomic replacement Collapsed at the end of replacement-local content Matches replacement authoring without selecting old content.
Formatting Preserve both endpoints and orientation The formatted content remains present and selected.
Paragraph split Collapsed at the start of the proposed right paragraph Enter continues in the paragraph it just created.
Paragraph merge Collapsed at the former paragraph boundary Backspace/Delete continues at the joined edge.

The native atomic document-fragment insertion inherits insertion’s end-of-new-content rule. It is shown here only as that rule’s consistency check; its proposal kind and WER v1 boundary were decided elsewhere.
